# WP-066: shaped laser pulses on the antenna

Launch pulses with a spectral phase (GDD, TOD, FOD) and with a focus made from a near field and wavefront aberration modes, from a self-contained pulse-shape module that can later move to a package of its own. It works when the shapes match their closed forms (the chirped Gaussian, the Airy envelope of a TOD, the sinc focus, Maréchal's Strehl ratio), the launched beam is the Yee propagation of its incident field, and the shapes' numbers have gradients that agree with finite differences.
