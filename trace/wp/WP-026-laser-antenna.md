# WP-026: the laser antenna

A laser antenna plane (A2) that launches a Gaussian beam with a given a₀, waist, duration, focus and polarization, with focus and chirp entering through the antenna's phase (A3). It works when a pulse in vacuum keeps the waist, Gouy phase and group velocity that the Yee dispersion predicts, and the energy it carries matches the Poynting flux through the antenna. Outcome: `reports/wp-026-laser-antenna.md`.
