# WP-069: the transposed pairs an adjoint of the step would use, as tested properties

Hold, by dot-product tests on random data, that `deposit!` is the transpose of `gather!` for every bins choice and N = 1, 2, 3, that the transpose of the Yee step with its current is the solver's own kernels with negated time arguments, and that the particles' read of E and B (plain and compensated) is the transpose of a deposit on the field layout. It works when each pair agrees to roundoff in every element type and a wrong partner (the step itself, another stagger) visibly fails.
