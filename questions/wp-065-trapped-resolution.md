# WP-065: open choices of the objective over every trapped electron

The owner chose the objective (every trapped electron, born or self-injected, at a fixed laser energy, over duration, waist, focus, delay and density). These are the choices the package made under it, each with the assumption it proceeded on. Files: `scripts/optim/trapped2d_case.jl`, `scripts/optim/trapped2d.jl`.

1. **Which electron counts as trapped.** A plasma electron has no label, so the choice is by its state. Options: a smooth weight in the forward momentum u_x; a weight in γ; a test on the wake's phase (an electron ahead of the separatrix). Assumed: a C² step in u_x from 3 to 5 (`TCASE.rise`), since the wake at 0.072 n_c moves with γ_φ ≈ 3.7 and the plasma's quiver stays below u_x ≈ 1 in WP-064's clean runs. Each run also reports J with the edges at (2, 4) and (4, 6).
2. **Where in the window.** WP-064's weight kept the first bucket (window x ≥ 12 to 24); the plasma's injected electrons sit behind it. Assumed: the weight rises from x = 4 to 10 (`TCASE.fade`), just ahead of the back band at λ₀/16 (it ends at 3.5), so the later buckets count; each run also reports the first bucket's J.
3. **The dopant under a density change.** Assumed: it stays at 1 % of the plasma's density (`TCASE.dopant_fraction`), so the density moves both.
4. **The refinement.** WP-064 refined only Δx (Δy = π/4 at every resolution). Assumed: Δx and Δy together (Δy = 2Δx, `ny = ppl/16`), with x-only runs at λ₀/32 to /64 beside them.
5. **The delay.** With the window's start and the read-out time fixed, the delay shifts the pulse against both, and at a duration of 6/ω₀ (about one cycle FWHM) it also shifts the carrier against the envelope's arrival in the plasma: at λ₀/8, J's central differences in the delay reach ForwardDiff's slope only below steps of about 10⁻³ (8 % off at a step of 0.2, T-892), a sign of structure on the scale of 1/ω₀. Assumed: the delay stays a parameter of the study; whether an optimizer should have it is for the report to say.
