# A7. Acceptance criteria and budgets per work package

Conservation residuals (Gauss's law to roundoff with Esirkepov, energy drift bound over N steps), agreement thresholds against the benchmark set, a throughput budget (nanoseconds per particle-step on the 4080 SUPER, within a stated factor of Smilei on the same card), memory budget (the `FunctionMap` harness doubles state; on 16 GB that caps 2D with P = 3 at roughly 10⁷ particles), and gradient-check tolerances.

How tolerances are set: measure, then propose; the owner accepts a proposed value by editing this file. Physics correctness is caught by tests against analytic solutions and conservation laws, held close to roundoff (a stated multiple of `eps(T)` scaled by the problem size and step count), not by loose agreement with other codes. Agreement thresholds for the benchmark set rest on the measured spread between Smilei and PIConGPU for equivalent inputs: ParticleMesh must agree with each of them to within that spread, and the spread is measured, never assumed. Finite-difference gradient checks have precision limits of their own, and no formula predicts them: the particle dynamics amplify rounding, so the floor is measured, never computed from `eps` and the step. Scan the step over at least five points (truncation error falls and cancellation error grows as the step shrinks), take the spread of the three neighbouring difference quotients around the minimum as the floor, and require the AD derivative to agree within a stated multiple of that spread, the multiple measured and proposed like every other tolerance here. Use common random numbers and the deterministic reference mode on both sides, and run the check in Float64 even where the production type is Float32.

Accepted thresholds:

- Noise-limited observables (the thermal cases `landau.toml` and `landau-oblique-2d.toml`): a code's ensemble rate lies within 2 jackknife standard errors of the linear theory of its own thermal load, and two codes are compared through that difference, within 2 combined standard errors √(σ₁² + σ₂²) at equal seed count. The Smilei–PIConGPU spread is not resolved from seed noise on these cases, so the statistical error sets the scale.
- Deterministic cross-code observables (the cold two-stream `growth_rate`): within 1 × 10⁻⁵ relative of each reference code, fitted over the reference run's output samples, with ParticleMesh's momenta loaded at t − Δt/2 as the reference codes load them (#226). A2's start holds for every other run.
- Cold cases against linear theory (two-stream, 2D Weibel): |γ − γ_ref| ≤ 1 × 10⁻³ θ, with θ the dispersion root and γ_ref = γ_lin + r_NL θ. γ_lin is the fit of the exact linear initial-value solution over the run's own samples, from the run's own start of the momenta; r_NL is the measured residual of those samples at the onset of saturation: −0.447 % (two-stream, 163 samples), −0.044 % (Weibel, mode) and −0.047 % (Weibel, energy). A change of a case, its cadence or its window rule needs a new scan.
- The throughput and memory budgets have no number, and none is proposed, until a performance optimization round has run.
